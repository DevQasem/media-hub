How do I get my cookies.txt? (step by step)
  1. On a computer, open Chrome, Edge or Firefox and log in to instagram.com with a spare (throwaway) account.
  2. Install the free extension “Get cookies.txt LOCALLY”.
  3. Click the extension’s icon, then press Export. If it asks for a format, choose Netscape.
  4. Press Choose file below and pick that cookies.txt - or paste the text into the box. Then press Save login.
  5. Do not press “Log out” on Instagram in that browser.
⚠ Please read
  • Use a throwaway account. Instagram can lock accounts that download a lot.
  • Don’t log out in that browser, or the cookies stop working.
